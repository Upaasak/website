SIX THINGS WE ARE TRYING TO GET GOOD AT

Areas, not departments. Every member works in at least two of them, and every area has to produce something a stranger can reproduce: a testbench, a report, a bitstream, a writeup.

R1 · FUNCTIONAL VERIFICATIONPRIMARY

Constrained-random and coverage-driven testbenches for blocks nobody has verified before. We care about the boring part: reproducible seeds, coverage that means something, and a signoff document that survives a hostile reader.

TOOLING
SystemVerilog · UVM
cocotb · Verilator
SVA · formal (open flows)
R2 · DSP FOR INSTRUMENTATIONACTIVE

Transforms and estimators that have to run in real time on a mid-range part: interpolated DFT, CORDIC magnitude and phase, rate-of-change estimation, decimation chains. Fixed point, error budgets written down before the RTL.

SEE ALSO
ipdft_channel_top ▸
R3 · TIMING & PHYSICAL CLOSUREACTIVE

Why the design missed by 180 ps, and what to do about it. Constraint hygiene, clock-domain crossings, retiming, floorplanning on FPGA and on open PDKs. Every closure is written up as a before/after with the actual reports attached.

TOOLING
Vivado · OpenSTA
OpenROAD · OpenLane
R4 · OPEN-SOURCE SILICONEXPLORATORY

Sky130 and open flows are the only route to real physical design for a lab with no licence budget. We push small blocks through GDS to learn where the flow lies to you, and publish the failures too.

TARGET
SKY130 · 130 nm
no tapeout yet

Two benches, shared boards, one JTAG server, regression running on whatever machine is free. Access is the resource we are actually pooling, so the scripts that manage it are treated as first-class work.
