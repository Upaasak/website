TICK → TRADE

A 100%-FABRIC HFT PIPELINE

When an exchange sends a price update, whoever answers first gets the trade. This pipeline makes the whole decision in an FPGA: an ITCH market message comes in one side and a finished OUCH order leaves the other, a fixed handful of clock cycles later. PS is control-plane only.

The parallel parser takes 8 bytes a cycle, the width of a 10G AXIS bus, and decodes every field of every message type in one cycle by speculating. Smaller, faster, and 12× lower latency. It assumes a 64-bit ingress from a 10G or 25G MAC.

Timed replay with LOBSTER-derived gaps: wire-to-trade median 13 / p99 96 cyc, ≈43 / 320 ns at 300 MHz. latency_ts is last-tick-wins, a lower-bound proxy under bursts.

THE PAYOFF

Order out, fill in, inventory updated, all over one established TCP session. The loop closes in simulation: market data, decision, SoupBinTCP egress through the TOE, execution report back through fill_rx.

Simulated, not traded. No exchange, no colocation, no money moved.

05 RISK & HONEST SCOPE

risk_gate is a hardware interlock. An order that breaks position or size limits is blocked in the circuit, not logged by software downstream.

▲ No exchange feed or colocation. Latency is sim datapath cycles, not live wire-to-trade.
